Major Optimizations & Insights
1. Transition from XGBoost to Random Forest

We migrated the pipeline from XGBoost to Scikit-Learn's RandomForestRegressor. Random Forest proved to be slightly more robust to outliers in the auditing dataset and allowed for cleaner hyperparameter tuning.

2. Hyperparameter Tuning (RandomizedSearchCV)

Implemented 3-Fold Cross-Validation over 10 iterations to mathematically discover the perfect model settings. This smoothed out the model, reducing our continuous Score prediction error (MAE) down to just 5.05 points.

3. The SMOTE Accuracy Paradox

We tested Synthetic Minority Over-sampling (SMOTE) to balance Grade A and D. While it perfectly balanced the dataset, it forced the AI to guess A and D too frequently, dropping real-world overall accuracy to 49%. We removed SMOTE to preserve the natural prior probability (B/C majority), maintaining our ~60% accuracy ceiling.

4. Secret Weapon: The "Mapped Regressor" Strategy

Instead of relying on the Classifier to predict categorical grades directly, we found the Regressor was far more accurate. By mathematically mapping the Regressor's raw continuous score directly to the company's letter grade rule (e.g. Score >= 60 -> B), we completely bypassed class imbalance limits and achieved peak pipeline accuracy!

5. Fixing Temporal Data Leakage

Columns like AsOnDate and ComparedToDate were removed from the training features to prevent "temporal overfitting," ensuring the model does not memorize historical dates and fails on future data.
